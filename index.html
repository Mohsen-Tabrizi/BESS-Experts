Service areas in detail

The following sections show how we work and what clients receive. Everything is structured so that owners, lenders and operators can act on it.

1) Independent Condition Monitoring

Condition monitoring is the continuous collection and analysis of operational and condition data from technical systems. The purpose is to detect performance losses and potential damage early, before they turn into costly downtime. This enables targeted maintenance planning, fewer outages and improved asset economics.

Our independent analysis provides a reliable decision basis for operators, asset managers, insurers and investors for safe, compliant and profitable operation of wind, PV and storage systems.

Why independent monitoring? Because independence is your advantage. We evaluate systems objectively without commercial interests and without contractual dependency on OEMs or service providers.

  • Continuous monitoring: real-time analysis to detect deviations early, from turbine to battery storage
  • Condition-based analyses: investigations for specific questions, such as before contract changes or at warranty expiry
  • Failure analysis: root cause investigations for performance losses or outages
  • Preventive services: avoid expensive damage and plan OPEX with asset lifetime in mind
  • Cross-system advisory: wind, PV, storage and grid connection, vendor-neutral and data-driven

Each engagement ends with a structured expert report and clear recommendations for correction, safety and optimization.

2) PV Monitoring and Operational Optimization

We deliver transparency over your PV plant from module string to the point of interconnection. Independent monitoring detects performance losses and malfunctions early and enables targeted interventions, stable grid integration and sustained yield.

Data-driven system analysis

We use existing SCADA data and, if needed, add sensor technology to get a complete view of energy yields, loss drivers, system behavior and environmental factors. Our analysis identifies patterns, deviations and anomalies and provides a reliable basis for fast measures and strategic optimization.

Reliable fault detection

Whether inverters, strings, transformers or communication interfaces, we identify typical failure sources early using historical benchmarks, temperature and voltage analysis and real-time alerts. This reduces downtime, increases operational safety and strengthens asset controlling.

Technical optimization and grid integration

We highlight technical weaknesses like incorrect controller settings, overloaded components or insufficient reactive power provision. We provide concrete improvement options and support compliance with grid technical requirements.

3) Data-based Monitoring and Analysis of Battery Energy Storage Systems

Battery systems are key building blocks of modern energy systems. With our vendor-neutral monitoring, you can keep operational parameters under control, remain warranty-compliant and prevent unplanned outages.

Intelligent analysis down to cell level

We integrate operational data into our analysis platform and combine existing SCADA data with additional sensor data if needed. This enables precise statements about cell temperature, charge and discharge cycles, voltage behavior and system response. Algorithms detect trends, deviations and potential failure sources for early corrective action.

Warranty-compliant and audit-proof

Warranty compliance is critical. Monitoring checks relevant parameters like cycles, temperature profiles and limit values and documents them in an audit-proof way. This supports warranty claims and communication with OEMs, aggregators and insurers.

Long-term profitability

A BESS is only valuable if it remains economically efficient over time. Monitoring enables verification that dispatch strategy and operation remain optimal. Inefficiencies can be identified and corrected with relevant stakeholders, protecting profitability in later operating years.

Early detection and outage prevention

We identify anomalies before they become critical. Temperature peaks, voltage deviations or faulty state of charge behavior are detected, flagged and assessed so you can act proactively.

4) Monitoring and Analysis of Grid Components and Power Quality

We monitor and analyze grid infrastructure to ensure reliability and efficiency from generation assets to the grid operator interface. Continuous monitoring detects anomalies and potential disturbances before they become costly failures.

Data integration and real-time monitoring

We integrate your SCADA system and can supplement infrastructure with measurement technology, such as partial discharge, insulation issues or critical temperature monitoring. Data is captured and processed in real time for decisions.

Power Quality Monitoring (PQM)

Stable voltage, stable frequency, limited harmonics. With PQM we evaluate electrical power quality at the grid interface, document deviations and reduce the risk of follow-on damage, supporting compliant operation and evidence for grid operators.

Avoiding typical weak points

Voltage dips, overloads and insulation faults are common. Our monitoring detects these early and helps resolve them.

Operational and investment security

Grid components must perform for years. Monitoring supports preventive maintenance, robust fault analysis and economic operation for wind, PV, storage and hybrid systems.

5) Photovoltaics: Due Diligence, Owner’s Engineering, Testing and Expert Reports

We are an independent partner for technical advisory, inspections and expert reporting for PV plants from design through construction and operations to repowering. We identify weak points, evaluate yield and safety risks and provide clear recommendations for stable cashflows across the lifecycle.

Technical Due Diligence (TDD)

  • Review of project documents, permits, contracts and concepts
  • Design and component compatibility assessment
  • Validation of yield forecasts, performance ratio and economics
  • Risk identification with clear mitigation recommendations
  • Structured, bankable reporting

Owner’s Engineering

  • Planning and design review and optimization
  • Tender documentation support
  • Construction supervision and quality control
  • Commissioning, acceptance tests and punch list management
  • Transparent documentation and progress reporting

DGUV V3 testing

Transfer stations are electrical operating rooms and require regular testing. We provide LV and HV test scope including measurements, functional checks and documented results.

Glare assessment

PV can cause glare for residents, roads, rail or airports. We simulate glare impact and provide authority-ready documentation plus mitigation recommendations.

Damage expert reports

Independent documentation, root cause analysis and court-ready reporting, optionally supported by thermography, IV curves and electroluminescence testing.

Yield assessment

Bankable yield reports based on recognized simulation methods and transparent loss factor modeling.

Drone services

Thermal inspections, orthophotos, construction progress documentation and AI-supported image evaluation with clear digital reporting.

6) Certification and Grid Integration

We support solar and storage projects through certification and verification needed for grid connection. Scope includes fault ride through tests, grid connection studies, protection testing and compliance evidence aligned with VDE-AR-N 4110/4120/4130 requirements.

  • Plant certification and prototype reports
  • Conformity verification after commissioning
  • Structured documentation for grid operators and authorities
7) HSE Advisory and Electrical Safety Organization

We support quality, health, safety and environment topics from planning through operation, onshore or offshore. We also help build a working electrical safety organization with clear roles, processes and evidence.

  • Roles and responsibilities: VEFK guidance and clear assignment logic
  • Electrical safety processes: switching and work instructions, lockout tagout, contractor control
  • Testing and maintenance organization: DGUV V3 concept, test registry, intervals, templates
  • Management systems and audits: ISO 9001/14001/45001 support, practical implementation
  • Risk assessments and HSE concepts: emergency plans, training, site rules, reporting
8) Storage technologies: Independent testing, DGUV V3 and Due Diligence

We verify battery storage systems for safety, contract compliance, accepted engineering practice and state of the art, from single projects to hybrid plants. The goal is technical reliability, regulatory compliance and long-term economic operability.

Independent testing for storage projects

  • Construction progress checks with detailed reporting and punch lists
  • Commissioning verification, including readiness confirmations where needed
  • Post-commissioning technical acceptance and contract compliance verification

DGUV V3 for storage plants and transfer stations

Recurring DGUV V3 testing improves safety and legal security. We provide LV and HV scope with protection relay checks, partial discharge measurement, lightning protection checks and documented results.

Due Diligence for BESS projects

We help investors and owners identify technical risks early by reviewing legal requirements, contracts, quality assurance, acceptance, warranty phase topics and operational performance.
