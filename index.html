Technical Services

Independent engineering support for BESS projects from concept to stable operation. BESS first, PV where relevant and always aligned with grid requirements.

Planning and design review

Review of system concept, sizing, architecture and interfaces. Focus on practical risks, buildability and lifecycle performance.

  • BESS integration in PV and hybrid plants
  • Single line and interface review
  • Risk register and mitigation actions

Configuration and integration review

Technical validation of control, protection and integration aspects across BMS, EMS, PCS and grid interface.

  • Grid support functions and settings
  • Operational logic and safety boundaries
  • Communication and monitoring readiness

Evidence documentation

Clear expert reporting with structured findings and actionable recommendations. Visual evidence can be added as a supporting layer when useful.

  • Expert technical reports
  • Photo and documentation package
  • Optional visual evidence documentation

Inspections & Verification

Independent inspections and recurring verification for operators, investors and manufacturers. Designed to reduce risk, protect warranties and increase reliability across the BESS lifecycle.

Delivery verification (Waren-Eingang)

Structured delivery inspection and documentation check to confirm scope, quality and completeness before installation.

  • Component and documentation verification
  • Traceability and compliance checks
  • Findings report with actions

Commissioning validation

Independent review of commissioning readiness, configuration and operational behavior at handover.

  • Functional checks and key parameters
  • Safety and operational readiness
  • Handover punch list and closeout

Periodic inspections (6–12 months)

Recurring inspections to verify performance, detect early issues and support warranty discussions.

  • Performance and reliability review
  • Warranty support and dispute clarification
  • Operator inspection plus manufacturer verification

Tender & Bid Support

Technical support for EPC contractors and bidders in BESS tenders, from document review to a clear basis for the bid decision.

Tender document review

Structured technical review of tender documents, Q&A and annexes before the bid.

  • Requirements, sizing and usable energy at the point of connection
  • Grid connection and interface matrix
  • Open-points list with status and sources

Supplier offer evaluation

Technical comparison of BESS and PCS supplier offers on a consistent basis.

  • Technical comparison of scope of supply and warranties
  • Review of performance and acceptance values (RTE, SOH, FAT/SAT)
  • Question lists for supplier clarifications and meetings

Bid decision support

A clear technical basis for go/no-go and pricing decisions.

  • Technical plausibility check of cost items and cost gaps
  • Work plan up to the bid deadline
  • Decision papers per project. Pricing and bid decisions remain with the client.

Development & Delivery Support (owner side)

Engineering support on the owner side, from early site screening through grid connection to construction and commissioning.

Site & permitting support

Early technical assessment of whether a site can be developed, connected and permitted.

  • Technical site screening: grid distance, layout and permitting feasibility, fatal flaws
  • Technical input for permit applications (electrical, fire protection, BImSchG screening)
  • Coordination of permit planners and environmental studies

Grid connection & compliance

Technical preparation and review of the grid connection up to certification.

  • Grid connection strategy (110 to 380 kV), point of connection and single-line diagrams
  • Reactive power and protection concepts, VDE-AR-N 4110 / 4120 / 4130
  • Coordination of grid studies and certification roadmap

Construction & commissioning (owner side)

Independent owner-side oversight during construction and handover.

  • Owner-side construction supervision and quality checks
  • Commissioning management and witnessing, FAT/SAT
  • Acceptance and handover support

About BESS Experts

BESS Experts provides technically grounded, manufacturer-neutral engineering support for BESS projects, including PV and grid integration. We support owners, investors, EPC contractors and manufacturers, from tender and grid connection through construction and commissioning to long-term operation.

Ingenieurbüro BESS EXPERTS is led by Mohsen Tabrizi, Ingenieur, with 20+ years of electrical engineering experience: PV manufacturing in the 2010s, CTO of GWD Energy GmbH (2024), Head of the Technical Department at Elektro Ghani (until 2025) and creator of a BESS Academy training programme (2025). The goal is simple: reduce technical risk, avoid avoidable failures, and support professional decisions across stakeholders.

How we work

  • Clear scope, clear deliverables, clear evidence
  • Lifecycle logic: evaluate, verify, operate, improve
  • One defined role per assignment: owner side or bidder side, never both on the same project
  • Confidential handling of all project data

BESS Experts does not carry out EPC, supply or installation work itself. Potential conflicts of interest are disclosed before any assignment.
